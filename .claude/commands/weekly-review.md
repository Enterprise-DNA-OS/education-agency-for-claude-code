---
description: The Monday review: attention list, the next intakes, money to claim and chase, slow payers, compliance and where enquiries come from.
---

Run `npm run agency -- weekly-review --json`.

Write the review in this order, short:
1. Three things to do this week, each with an owner.
2. Intakes in the next eight weeks: who is not ready and why.
3. Money: claimable now by institution, overdue invoices, the slowest payer.
4. Compliance: any BREACH line, with the rule. Say that a clean check means the records are complete, not that the law is met.
5. Enquiries: which source converts best, which is costing counsellor time for little.

Save it to `drafts/weekly-review-<date>.md` if asked. Nothing sends.
